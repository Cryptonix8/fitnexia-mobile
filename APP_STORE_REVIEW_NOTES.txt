App Store Review Notes — Fitnexia
=================================

Guideline 4 — Design (Sign in / Register)
----------------------------------------
• Sign in and account creation are fully in-app:
  - Email / password on native Login and Register screens
  - Sign in with Apple (native)
  - Google Sign-In via the native Google SDK (no system Safari for Fitnexia account creation)
• Account deletion: Profile → "Cerrar cuenta" (athlete, instructor, and gym profiles).

Guideline 3.1.1 — In-App Purchase
--------------------------------
• Fitnexia platform SaaS subscriptions on iOS are sold only via Apple In-App Purchase:
  - Instructor Pro: fitnexia.instructor.pro.monthly
  - Gym Professional: fitnexia.gym.professional.monthly
  - Gym Premium: fitnexia.gym.premium.monthly
  - Gym Enterprise: fitnexia.gym.enterprise.monthly
• Screens: Instructor → Perfil → Plan / Comisión; Gym → Perfil → Plan Fitnexia.
• "Restaurar compras de Apple" is available on those screens.
• Mercado Pago is NOT used on iOS to purchase Fitnexia digital subscriptions.

Mercado Pago (real-world / marketplace only)
--------------------------------------------
Displayed with in-app browser (ASWebAuthenticationSession / Safari View Controller), not the
system Safari app, for:
• Athlete class bookings (real-world fitness instruction)
• Court reservations (physical facility)
• Club membership dues (gym socios)
• Seller payout onboarding ("Conectar cuenta de cobros") — not Fitnexia account registration
